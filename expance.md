## 🗂️ Categorized Expense Breakdown (For Feed & OPEX Module)

### 1. Feed, Fodder & Nutrition (ખોરાક અને પૂરક આહાર)
* **Khod - Bhusu (ખોળ અને ભૂસું)**: Concentrates, cottonseed/mustard cake, and dry wheat straw.
* **Tuver Chunni (તુવેર ચૂની)**: High-protein legume pulses husk feed.
* **Green Charo (લીલો ચારો)**: In-house farm-grown green fodder (Maize, Lucerne, Napier).
* **Bear Charo (બહારનો ચારો)**: Purchased external fodder / bulk grass deliveries.
* **Sarso Nu Tel (સરસવનું તેલ - Mustard Oil)**: Energy supplement for lactation, fat boost, digestion, and coat health.
* **Black Salt (સંચળ / સિંધવ મીઠું)**: Mineral digestion lick and electrolyte booster for high-yielding buffaloes and cows.

### 2. Labor & Workforce (મજૂરી અને સ્ટાફ ખર્ચ)
* **Daily Labour for Cutting Charo (ઘાસ કાપવાની દૈનિક મજૂરી)**: Daily wage workers for fodder harvesting, transport, and chaffing.
* **Monthly Salary (સ્ટાફનો માસિક પગાર)**: Monthly wages for permanent shed milkers, cleaners, and farm hands.

### 3. Utilities & Farm Connectivity (વીજળી અને બિલ)
* **Light Bill / Electricity (લાઇટ બિલ / વીજળી)**: Power for chillers, milking machines, chaff cutter, water pumps, and shed fans.
* **Internet Bill (ઇન્ટરનેટ બિલ)**: High-speed Wi-Fi for IoT herd monitoring, CCTV surveillance, and cloud sync.

### 4. Veterinary & Herd Healthcare (તબીબી અને આરોગ્ય સારવાર)
* **Madicin (દવાઓ)**: Antibiotics, mastitis tubes, calcium gels, deworming, and seasonal vaccines.
* **Doctor Fees (પશુ ડૉક્ટર ફી)**: Veterinary consultation, emergency visits, and artificial insemination (AI) charges.

### 5. Hardware & Shed Maintenance (સાધનો અને જાળવણી)
* **Chain (સાંકળ - Animal Tying Chains)**: Heavy-duty tethering chains, hooks, and stall fittings.

### 6. Loans & Capital Financing (ધિરાણ અને હપ્તા)
* **EMI (લોન / ઇન્સ્ટોલમેન્ટ)**: Monthly bank EMI for buffalo herd acquisition, machinery, or shed construction.

### 7. Custom Categories & Other Expenses (કસ્ટમ કેટેગરી - વપરાશકર્તા દ્વારા ગમે તે નામ)
* **Custom Category Functionality**: Users can define and type **any name** for their own custom categories (e.g. `Diesel & Machinery`, `Shed Construction`, `Transportation`, `Silage Bagging`, `Land Lease`, `Organic Fertilizer`, `Legal & Tax`).
* Custom categories automatically generate their own filter pills in the Expense Ledger and can be selected or created on the fly in the "Log New Expense" modal.
